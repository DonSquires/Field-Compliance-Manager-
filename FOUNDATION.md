## MISSION — why we exist

**We exist to encourage and ensure accountability: to record it honestly,
preserve it faithfully, and prove it when it matters.**

Everyone who acts is answerable for what they do: the officer to their
organisation, to those they serve, to the law, and to the people they meet; the
organisation to its jurisdiction and its community; the landowner for what
happens on their land. This app exists to record those responsibilities
honestly, preserve them faithfully, and prove them when it matters — to a
standard that stands in court. We never fabricate, because a broken chain of
accountability is a broken promise.

Compliance is not our purpose. Accountability is. Compliance is simply what
accountability looks like when it is done well.

---

## PURPOSE — what we do about it

We work to prevent harm and damage where we can, and to support those affected
when it has already occurred.

**People before property.**

---

## THE HOW — the method that makes the purpose real

Accountability operates across the *timeline of harm*. Every capability in this
app is one moment on that timeline:

1. **Prevent** — presence and readiness that keep a future victim from ever
   becoming one.
2. **Interrupt** — act on a breach before harm lands, on behalf of the party the
   rule exists to protect.
3. **Establish, Assess & Remedy** — where harm has landed, establish the offence
   *at the harmed party*, measure its severity *there*, identify the subject, and
   apply a proportionate remedy.
4. **Prove** — record honestly, preserve faithfully, and make it provable when it
   matters.

---

## THE ANCHOR RULE — how this becomes true in the system, not just in words

- The **harmed party** (present or future) is the anchor of the accountability.
  The offence is established where the harm lands, not where it is convenient to
  record it.
- The **subject** — the offender, the vehicle, or the place producing the harm —
  is anchored separately from the harmed party.
- The **operator is the custodian of the record, not the owner of the
  accountability.**
- **People before property**, in the system as in the words: accountability
  anchors to the harmed party and the place of harm.

**Corollary — accountability outlives the contract.** Because the operator is
custodian and not owner, a client is never disadvantaged by changing provider.
The client-owned operational truth (the place's record of what happened) stays
with the harmed party and the place of harm; it does not belong to the departing
operator and does not transfer to the new one — it never moved, only the
custodian changed. Provider-private working data (commercial terms, margins,
internal method) belongs to that operator and is never shared. The property line
is *ownership*, not data type. *(Authored: Don Squires, 2026-08-08.)*

This anchor rule is universal. It applies to every module and every future
design decision. Bringing the system into full conformance is staged work, but
the standard itself is not staged.

## THE KNOWN GAP — the work this foundation reveals

Today, much of the system still anchors accountability records to the operating
organisation rather than the harmed party and the place of harm. Closing that
gap is core remaining work, and this document is the test that work must satisfy.

---

## THE KEEPER — the human reason beneath all of it

> **"The custodian is the keeper of the brothers."**
> — Rebekah Squires

The entire foundation of security is *I am my brother's keeper.* The custodian
keeps the record for those who cannot keep it themselves — the harmed, the
absent, the future. To hold that record faithfully is to keep faith with them.
Everything above — the timeline of harm, the anchor rule, custodian not owner —
is only the engineering of how we keep our brother.
